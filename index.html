About CDS Cold

Solutions That Fit Your Needs. Service That Builds Trust.

CDS Cold is a family-owned and operated cold storage facility proudly serving Santa Fe Springs, California. We provide refrigerated storage, packing, and distribution services that help bring quality produce to stores throughout the region.

What sets us apart is simple: we treat every shipment with the care and attention that only a family business can deliver. Your success is our success, and we're committed to building long-term partnerships based on reliability, quality, and trust.
